Tips for an Accurate Screening Photo

For best results, upload a clear, well-lit image with the nail(s) fully visible. Whole-hand images are supported. Avoid blurry, very dark, distant, or heavily obstructed images.

  • Hold your hand steady on a stable flat surface to prevent camera blur.
  • Ensure balanced, natural lighting; avoid direct camera flash or deep shadows.
  • Both close-up photos of an individual nail and whole-hand images containing multiple nails are supported.
  • Ensure nails are clean and free of polish or heavy decorative coatings.
